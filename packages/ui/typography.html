A page can hold more than notes

A workspace can bring documents, structured data and small pieces of information together. Use clear headings to create a path through the content, and let paragraphs stay comfortable to scan.

Keep labels quieter than the content they describe. Use weight and spacing to establish hierarchy before introducing color.
